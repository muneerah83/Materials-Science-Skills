# Thermal Insulation — Figure Rules

## Technical requirements per figure type
- **Thermal conductivity vs density/porosity**: measurement method (hot-plate, hot-wire, laser flash, heat-flow meter) and mean temperature stated; cannot compare values across studies without same method
- **Thermal conductivity vs temperature at humidity levels**: humidity conditioning protocol stated; temperature range covers service range
- **Compressive stress-strain curves**: density labelled per curve; strain rate stated
- **Pore size distribution**: measurement method (mercury intrusion, micro-CT, N₂ adsorption) stated; cumulative and differential curves shown
- **Hygrothermal aging curves**: conditioning parameters (RH, temperature, duration) stated; conductivity or strength at each checkpoint
- **Dual y-axis (thermal + mechanical)**: only when two properties are from the same specimens; axes must share the same x-variable

## Reviewer-sensitive pitfalls
- ⚠️ Thermal conductivity compared across studies without stating measurement method and mean temperature.
- ⚠️ Compressive strength without density — insulation properties are density-dependent.
- ⚠️ Fire performance data (LOI, cone calorimetry) without specimen geometry and test standard.
- ⚠️ Dual y-axis mixing properties from different specimens.
