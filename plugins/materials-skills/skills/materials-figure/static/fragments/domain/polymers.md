# Polymers — Figure Rules

## Technical requirements per figure type
- **DSC thermogram**: heating/cooling rate stated; endo-up or endo-down convention labelled; use 2nd heating cycle unless 1st-cycle history is the subject
- **TGA/DTG curve**: atmosphere (N₂ or air) and heating rate stated; report onset temperature and peak degradation temperature
- **Stress-strain curve**: full curve required (not just summary bar chart); report both strength and elongation
- **DMA plot (E′, E″, tan δ)**: heating rate and frequency stated; Tg taken from tan δ peak, not E′ onset
- **SEM cross-section**: scale bar; control vs modified panels at same magnification; filler/matrix boundary indicated
- **Rheology curve**: temperature or frequency sweep range stated; log scale for viscosity; key transitions annotated

## Reviewer-sensitive pitfalls
- ⚠️ DSC/TGA without heating rate or atmosphere — values cannot be compared across studies.
- ⚠️ Mechanical improvement claimed with bar charts only — stress-strain curves required.
- ⚠️ DMA without frequency or heating rate specification.
- ⚠️ SEM at single magnification or without control comparison.
- ⚠️ Missing error bars on mechanical summary data.

## Caption boundary phrases
- `DSC thermograms of [polymer] (2nd heating cycle, [rate]°C/min). Tg = [value]°C; Tm = [value]°C.`
- `TGA/DTG curves under [N₂/air] atmosphere at [rate]°C/min. Degradation onset: [value]°C; peak: [value]°C.`
- `Representative stress-strain curves of [formulations] at [condition]. Crosshead speed: [value] mm/min.`
- `SEM micrographs of fracture surface: (a) [neat polymer], (b) [composite with filler content]. Scale bar = [value] µm.`
