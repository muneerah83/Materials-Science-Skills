# Functional / Electronic Materials — Figure Rules

## Technical requirements per figure type
- **J-V / I-V curve**: forward and reverse scan both shown; dark and illuminated curves if relevant; temperature stated; scan rate noted
- **UV-vis absorption**: Tauc plot inset for bandgap; direct vs indirect assumption explicitly stated; baseline correction noted
- **PL spectrum**: excitation wavelength stated; normalised or absolute intensity — must be consistent within comparison
- **XRD pattern**: phase labels; substrate peak identified and labelled; FWHM reported for crystallinity quality claim
- **EIS (Nyquist/Bode)**: frequency range stated; temperature noted; equivalent circuit model shown with fitted component values
- **Cyclic voltammogram**: scan rate stated; reference electrode identified; potential window labelled
- **Device performance (PCE, EQE)**: active area stated; illumination intensity and spectrum noted; statistics across ≥ 5 devices

## Reviewer-sensitive pitfalls
- ⚠️ J-V curve without scan direction or rate — hysteresis index cannot be assessed.
- ⚠️ Bandgap from Tauc plot without stating direct/indirect assumption.
- ⚠️ Solar cell efficiency without device area or device count statistics.
- ⚠️ EIS without equivalent circuit fit or frequency range.
- ⚠️ PL intensity compared across samples without identical measurement conditions.

## Caption boundary phrases
- `J-V characteristics of [device] under [illumination/dark] at [temperature]. Scan direction: [forward/reverse]; scan rate: [value] V/s.`
- `UV-vis absorption spectra of [material] with Tauc plot (inset) for [direct/indirect] bandgap: [value] eV.`
- `Nyquist plots of [material] at [temperature]. Symbols: experimental; lines: equivalent circuit fit ([circuit]).`
- `Statistical distribution of [PCE/EQE] across [n] devices. Box: 25th–75th percentile; whiskers: min–max.`
