# Metals and Alloys — Family Reviewer Template
# Applies to all metals sub-domains. Load the matching delta/ file for sub-domain-specific criteria.

## Shared review axes (all metals systems)

- **Grain size**: Is grain size reported with the measurement method (ASTM E112 or equivalent)?
  "Grain refinement" claimed without measured grain size is not acceptable.
- **Heat treatment completeness**: Are temperature, dwell time, AND cooling rate all reported?
  Cooling rate is the most critical parameter — its omission prevents reproducibility.
- **Strength-ductility balance**: Are both strength AND elongation/ductility reported?
  Strength-only data cannot assess the trade-off or fracture risk.
- **Fracture analysis**: Are fracture mechanism claims supported by SEM fractography?
  Inferring fracture mode from the stress-strain curve alone is insufficient.
- **Corrosion/environment**: Is the test standard, electrolyte composition, and exposure duration specified?

## Common fatal flaws (all metals systems)

| Flaw | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Grain refinement claimed without grain size measurement | Unverifiable mechanism |
| Heat treatment without cooling rate | Cannot reproduce or compare |
| Strength reported without ductility | Incomplete characterization |
| Fracture mechanism from tensile curve only, no fractography | Circular reasoning |
| Corrosion test without electrolyte or duration | Results cannot be compared |

## Shared reviewer questions

- "What is the grain size and how was it measured (linear intercept / ASTM E112)?"
- "What is the cooling rate after heat treatment?"
- "How does the strength-ductility combination compare with baseline or existing grades?"
- "What is the specimen orientation relative to the processing/rolling direction?"
- "Is fractography provided to support the fracture mechanism claim?"
