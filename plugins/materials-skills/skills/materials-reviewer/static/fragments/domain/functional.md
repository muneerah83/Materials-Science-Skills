# Functional / Electronic Materials — Family Reviewer Template
# Applies to all functional sub-domains. Load the matching delta/ file for sub-domain-specific criteria.

## Shared review axes (all functional systems)

- **Device statistics**: n devices AND efficiency/property distribution must be reported.
  Reporting only the best device is not acceptable; reviewers require statistical spread.
- **Measurement conditions**: Temperature, frequency, illumination intensity, scan rate — all must be stated.
  Properties of functional materials are strongly condition-dependent; unreported conditions = irreproducible.
- **Stability characterization**: Test duration AND retention percentage both required.
  "Stable" without a duration and retention rate is an unverifiable claim.
- **Benchmark comparison**: Results must be compared with state-of-the-art for the same material class.
  Performance claims without literature benchmark cannot establish competitiveness.
- **Mechanism evidence**: Defect, interface, or doping claims must be supported by direct characterization (XPS, TEM, EPR, TRPL), not just device output data.

## Common fatal flaws (all functional systems)

| Flaw | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Best device only, no statistics across devices | Reviewer will flag as cherry-picked |
| Stability from unrealistically short duration | Durability claim not established |
| Measurement conditions unreported (T, frequency, illumination) | Results not reproducible or comparable |
| No benchmark against state-of-the-art | Cannot assess competitiveness |
| Mechanism attributed to material without contact/interface consideration | Confounded attribution |

## Shared reviewer questions

- "How many devices were measured? What is the efficiency distribution (mean ± SD)?"
- "What is the stability test duration and the retention percentage at end-of-test?"
- "What were the measurement temperature, frequency, and illumination intensity?"
- "How does this performance compare with the current state-of-the-art for this material class?"
- "Is the mechanism evidence from direct characterization (XPS/TEM) or inferred from device output?"
