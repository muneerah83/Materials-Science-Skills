# Tier 1 — Domain Journal Review Standard
# Target: CBM, CCC, JBE, RMPD, Ceramics International, Energy & Buildings, CSCM, JRE
# Scope: Domain-specific engineering/materials journals; incremental advances in established systems

## Novelty bar

Incremental improvement within an established system is acceptable novelty for this tier.
"First study of X modifier in Y matrix" is sufficient if X and Y are clearly defined
and the gap is plausible. Full systematic literature evidence is not required,
but the gap statement must not contradict well-known prior work.

## Mechanism evidence

Qualitative characterization evidence is sufficient:
- FTIR: peak identification and qualitative comparison
- SEM: morphology observation and description
- XRD: phase identification and peak position

Quantitative mechanism (kinetics, activation energy, R²-backed correlation) strengthens
the paper but is not a rejection criterion at this tier.

## Statistics minimum

- n ≥ 3 replicates per group
- Mean ± SD reported for all mechanical/performance claims
- "Significant" requires at least a percentage difference with error bars that do not overlap;
  formal ANOVA or t-test is recommended but not mandatory
- COV (coefficient of variation) expected for geotechnical and concrete data

## Comparison scope

Comparison vs. internal control or unmodified baseline is sufficient.
State-of-the-art comparison with literature is beneficial but not required.

## Figure quality

- Readable axis labels, correct units, legible font size
- Error bars on all performance plots
- SEM scale bar present
- XRD reference peak positions identified

## Desk-reject risk indicators at this tier

- No control group
- No replicate statistics
- Mechanism claimed from performance alone (circular reasoning)
- Test method not cited
- Overclaims field-readiness without field evidence
