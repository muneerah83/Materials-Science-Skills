## Domain-specific review axes

- **Substrate effect correction in nanoindentation**: Hardness and modulus from nanoindentation
  are valid only when indentation depth < 10% of film thickness (Bückle rule).
  Data at deeper indentation without substrate correction reports composite film+substrate values.
- **Residual stress method**: Curvature method (Stoney equation) OR XRD sin²ψ method — both acceptable,
  but the method must be stated. Sign (tensile vs. compressive) matters for adhesion and cracking.
- **Adhesion quantification**: Scratch test critical load (Lc) with failure mode classification required.
  Qualitative tape test (pass/fail) is not accepted as adhesion evidence in peer-reviewed journals.
- **Film uniformity**: "Uniform deposition" requires thickness mapping across the substrate area,
  not just a single-point measurement.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- Nanoindentation H/E without substrate correction — reports composite rather than film properties.
- Residual stress direction (tensile/compressive) not reported.
- Adhesion from tape test only — quantitative critical load absent.
- Uniformity claimed from a single measurement point.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Is nanoindentation depth < 10% of film thickness? Is substrate correction applied?"
- "What is the residual stress sign (tensile or compressive) and measurement method?"
- "Is the scratch test critical load Lc reported with failure mode classification?"
- "Is film thickness uniformity mapped across the deposition area?"
