# Geotechnical Materials — Reviewer Delta
# Adds to the civil-template. Load civil.md first, then this file.

## Domain-specific review axes

- **UCS curing age**: Compressive strength must be reported at multiple curing ages (7, 28 days minimum).
  Single-age UCS cannot characterize strength development.
- **Soil variability**: Replicate statistics (coefficient of variation) are especially critical given natural soil heterogeneity.
  n < 3 for any geotechnical claim is insufficient.
- **Permeability comparison**: Grouting effectiveness requires before/after hydraulic conductivity data (ASTM D5084).
  Permeability from a single measurement point is not acceptable.
- **Stress-strain behavior**: Peak strength alone is insufficient; full stress-strain curve and E50 modulus required.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- UCS from a single curing age only — no strength development curve.
- Soil stabilization claimed from a single binder dosage — no dosage optimization.
- Permeability claim without before/after comparison.
- Anisotropy of soil hydraulic conductivity ignored.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Is the UCS reported at multiple curing ages to show strength development?"
- "How many replicates? What is the COV given soil heterogeneity?"
- "Is hydraulic conductivity reported before and after grouting?"
- "Is the full stress-strain curve (with E50) provided, not just peak strength?"
