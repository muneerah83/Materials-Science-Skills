## Domain-specific review axes

- **FTIR curing evidence**: The 915 cm⁻¹ epoxide C–O peak is the primary curing indicator.
  A "curing confirmed" claim requires a quantified peak ratio change, not a qualitative shift.
- **Dosage optimum**: The dosage-performance curve MUST show the descending branch.
  A curve that only rises cannot establish an optimum — the true peak may be outside the tested range.
- **Morphology**: SEM at optimal dosage should show sea-island morphology; co-continuous network at higher loading.
  Phase-separation evidence supports the claimed dosage window.
- **Rheology**: G' crossover (gel point) is required to confirm network formation for mechanism claims.
- **Storage stability**: Emulsion stability test (ASTM D6930 / GB/T 20623) required alongside performance.
  A material that cannot be stored cannot be applied in the field.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- Dosage curve without descending branch — optimum cannot be established.
- "Curing confirmed" from FTIR without quantified epoxide peak conversion ratio.
- No storage stability data alongside bonding performance claims.
- Rheology G' crossover missing for network formation claims.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Does the dosage-performance curve include the descending branch?"
- "What is the epoxide peak conversion ratio (915 cm⁻¹) at the claimed optimum dosage?"
- "What is the storage stability of the emulsion at the target dosage?"
- "Is the G'/G'' crossover point reported to confirm network formation?"
