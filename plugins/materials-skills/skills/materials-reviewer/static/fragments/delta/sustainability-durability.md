# Sustainability and Durability — Reviewer Delta
# Adds to the civil-template. Load civil.md first, then this file.

## Domain-specific review axes

- **LCA requirements**: "Green", "low-carbon", or "sustainable" claims require LCA per ISO 14040/14044.
  Mandatory: functional unit, system boundary (cradle-to-gate/grave), and all relevant impact categories.
  CO₂ reduction percentages without a reference system and scope statement are rejected.
- **Coupled degradation**: Durability claims under a single degradation mechanism (e.g., freeze-thaw only)
  are insufficient if the service environment involves coupling (freeze-thaw + chloride, carbonation + moisture).
- **Service-life model**: Extrapolating accelerated lab test results to service life requires a validated model.
  Lab durability ≠ predicted service life without a degradation model and exposure correlation.
- **Waste valorization**: Leaching/toxicity data (heavy metals, organics) is required before claiming
  environmental benefit from waste incorporation.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- "Low-carbon" or "green" without LCA functional unit and system boundary.
- Cherry-picked LCA impact categories without full disclosure of all categories.
- Single-mechanism durability claimed without addressing coupled degradation.
- Waste valorization without leaching/toxicity assessment.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Is the LCA functional unit defined? What is the system boundary?"
- "Which impact categories are included? Are excluded categories justified?"
- "Is coupled degradation (e.g., chloride + freeze-thaw) addressed?"
- "Is leaching toxicity of the waste material assessed?"
