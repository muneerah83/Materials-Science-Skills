# Refractory Materials — Reviewer Delta
# Adds to the ceramics-template. Load ceramics.md first, then this file.

## Domain-specific review axes

- **Thermal shock — residual strength curve**: A single thermal shock cycle result is insufficient.
  Residual strength vs. number of thermal cycles (curve) is required to characterize degradation rate.
  Critical temperature difference (ΔTc) must also be reported.
- **Hot strength for service claims**: Cold compressive strength (CCS) at room temperature
  cannot substitute for hot modulus of rupture (HMOR) at service temperature for any
  high-temperature mechanical performance claim.
- **Slag corrosion cross-section**: Qualitative corrosion observation or corrosion depth alone
  is insufficient. SEM/EDS cross-section elemental mapping AND quantitative corrosion depth required.
  XRD phase analysis of the reaction layer is expected.
- **Water quench vs. air quench**: Thermal shock resistance from water quench cannot be directly
  applied to predict air-quench or service-condition thermal shock performance.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- Thermal shock resistance from single cycle without residual strength vs. cycles curve.
- High-temperature claims based on cold CCS only — HMOR not measured.
- Slag corrosion without cross-section SEM/EDS and quantitative penetration depth.
- Water quench results presented as representative of service thermal shock.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Is the residual strength vs. number of thermal cycles curve provided?"
- "Is hot modulus of rupture (HMOR) at service temperature measured?"
- "Does slag corrosion include SEM/EDS elemental mapping and quantitative depth?"
- "What quench medium was used? Can results be translated to service conditions?"
