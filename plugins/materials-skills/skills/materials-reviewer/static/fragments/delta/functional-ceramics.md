# Functional Ceramics (Piezoelectric, Dielectric) — Reviewer Delta
# Adds to the ceramics-template. Load ceramics.md first, then this file.

## Domain-specific review axes

- **d33 measurement mode**: Quasi-static d33 meter (Berlincourt) and IEEE 180 resonance method give
  different values. The method and poling conditions (field, temperature, time) must be stated.
- **P-E hysteresis loop**: Required for any ferroelectric or piezoelectric claim.
  Must report Pr (remanent polarization), Ec (coercive field), and Psat (saturation). Applied field must reach saturation.
- **Frequency dependence**: Dielectric constant and loss tangent at a single frequency are insufficient.
  Data at ≥ 3 frequencies is required to characterize relaxation behavior and loss mechanisms.
- **Curie temperature**: Required for any temperature-stability claim.
  DSC or dielectric vs. temperature measurement must locate Tc.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- d33 reported without measurement method or poling conditions.
- P-E loop that does not reach saturation — no valid Pr or Ec.
- Dielectric data at only one frequency — relaxation not characterized.
- Temperature stability claimed without Curie temperature measurement.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "What method was used for d33 (Berlincourt quasi-static or IEEE 180 resonance)?"
- "Are the P-E loop poling conditions (field, temperature, time) reported?"
- "Is dielectric constant measured at ≥ 3 frequencies to characterize relaxation?"
- "Is the Curie temperature measured and reported for temperature-stability claims?"
