## Domain-specific review axes

- **Retained austenite**: When quench-and-temper processing is claimed, retained austenite quantification
  by XRD or EBSD is required. Retained austenite affects dimensional stability and fatigue.
- **Precipitation strengthening**: TEM evidence of precipitate size, distribution, and coherency is required.
  Hardness evolution curve alone does not confirm the strengthening mechanism.
- **Weldability**: Welding procedure qualification data (parameters, consumables, heat input) must be provided.
  Claiming "good weldability" from mechanical data alone is insufficient.
- **Corrosion**: Test electrolyte composition (pH, chloride concentration) must be stated.
  Passive film behavior differs markedly between dilute and concentrated chloride solutions.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- Retained austenite not quantified in quenched-tempered microstructures.
- Precipitation strengthening claimed without TEM precipitate characterization.
- Weld performance without fracture location (base metal / HAZ / weld metal) documentation.
- Stainless steel corrosion without defining the electrolyte.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Is retained austenite quantified? Does it affect dimensional stability under service loading?"
- "What TEM evidence (precipitate size, distribution, coherency) supports the precipitation mechanism?"
- "Are welding procedure parameters (heat input, preheat, filler) documented?"
- "What is the electrolyte composition for corrosion measurements?"
