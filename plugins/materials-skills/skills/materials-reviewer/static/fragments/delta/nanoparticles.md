## Domain-specific review axes

- **Toxicity dose-response**: Any biocompatibility or cytotoxicity claim requires dose-response
  curve AND IC50 value across multiple cell lines (ISO 10993-5).
  Single-concentration viability from one cell line is not accepted.
- **SPR peak interpretation**: For metallic NPs (Au, Ag), FWHM of the SPR peak correlates with
  size distribution breadth. Narrow, symmetric SPR indicates monodisperse particles.
- **Catalytic performance**: Turnover frequency (TOF) per active site is the required metric
  for catalytic efficiency claims. Conversion rate per gram cannot compare catalysts with different loadings.
- **Synthesis scalability**: "Scalable synthesis" claims require yield and size-distribution data
  at multiple reaction scales, not just milligram-scale lab synthesis.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- Cytotoxicity from single concentration, single cell line — dose-response absent.
- Catalytic efficiency from conversion rate only, without TOF normalization.
- Scalability claimed from single-batch milligram-scale synthesis.
- Colloidal stability from single pH/ionic strength measurement.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Is the cytotoxicity dose-response curve provided across at least two cell lines?"
- "Is catalytic efficiency normalized as TOF per active site?"
- "Is colloidal stability measured across the relevant pH and ionic strength range?"
- "Is synthesis reproducibility demonstrated across multiple batches and scales?"
