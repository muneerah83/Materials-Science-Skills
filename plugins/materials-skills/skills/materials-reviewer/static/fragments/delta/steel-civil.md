# Steel in Civil Engineering — Reviewer Delta
# Adds to the civil-template. Load civil.md first, then this file.

## Domain-specific review axes

- **Corrosion assessment completeness**: Corrosion potential (Ecorr) alone is insufficient.
  Polarization resistance (Rp) or corrosion rate (mm/year from EIS/weight-loss) must accompany potential data.
- **Weld performance**: HAZ hardness profile AND fracture location (base metal / HAZ / weld metal)
  both required for weld joint claims.
- **Structural code compliance**: Yield strength claims should reference the applicable design code grade
  (e.g., GB/T 1591 Q355, ASTM A572 Gr50) for structural adequacy assessment.
- **Coating adhesion**: Pull-off or cross-cut adhesion test required alongside any corrosion protection claim.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- Corrosion potential reported without polarization resistance or rate — potential alone has no quantitative meaning.
- Weld strength without HAZ hardness profile or fracture location identification.
- Coating protection claimed without adhesion test data.
- Short-term lab corrosion extrapolated to service life without a validated model.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Is corrosion rate (mm/year) provided from EIS or weight-loss measurement?"
- "Is the HAZ hardness profile shown across the weld cross-section?"
- "Where did fracture occur: base metal, HAZ, or weld metal?"
- "Is coating adhesion quantified by pull-off or cross-cut test?"
