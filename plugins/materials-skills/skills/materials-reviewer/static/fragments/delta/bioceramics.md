# Bioceramics and Bioactive Glasses — Reviewer Delta
# Adds to the ceramics-template. Load ceramics.md first, then this file.

## Domain-specific review axes

- **SBF bioactivity protocol**: Simulated body fluid immersion must follow ISO 23317.
  Non-standard SBF composition or ion concentrations invalidate apatite formation claims.
- **Apatite formation as time series**: A single immersion time point is not accepted.
  XRD and SEM evidence at multiple time points (e.g., 3, 7, 14, 28 days) is required.
- **Biocompatibility boundary**: In vitro cytotoxicity (ISO 10993-5) on ONE cell line
  does NOT establish in vivo biocompatibility. This extrapolation must not be made.
- **In vitro to in vivo degradation**: In vitro mass-loss kinetics cannot be directly extrapolated
  to in vivo degradation rate without explicit justification.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- Bioactivity claimed without ISO 23317 SBF protocol compliance.
- Apatite formation shown at one time point only.
- Single-cell-line cytotoxicity presented as "biocompatible" for clinical claims.
- In vitro degradation directly extrapolated to in vivo without validation caveat.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Does the SBF experiment follow ISO 23317 (ion concentrations, temperature, volume)?"
- "Is apatite formation shown across multiple immersion time points?"
- "Is in vitro cytotoxicity from multiple cell lines, or only one?"
- "Are in vitro degradation results appropriately caveated against in vivo translation?"
