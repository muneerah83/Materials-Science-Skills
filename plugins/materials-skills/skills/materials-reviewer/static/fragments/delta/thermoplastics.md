## Domain-specific review axes

- **Moisture conditioning**: ASTM D618 conditioning is required before testing hygroscopic polymers
  (nylon/PA, PC, PET, PEEK). Uncontrolled moisture can reduce tensile strength by 20–40%.
- **Crystallinity measurement**: If crystallinity effects are claimed, crystallinity index from DSC
  (ASTM D3418) or XRD is required — not just claimed from processing conditions.
- **Melt flow consistency**: For reprocessing or recyclability claims, MFI (ASTM D1238) before
  and after each processing cycle is required to track molecular weight degradation.
- **Orientation effects**: For injection-moulded or extruded specimens, flow direction matters.
  Tensile specimens from different locations/orientations should be compared.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- Hygroscopic polymer mechanical data without conditioning — moisture state unknown.
- Crystallinity claimed without DSC or XRD measurement.
- Recyclability claimed without MFI data across processing cycles.
- No consideration of flow-induced orientation in injection-moulded specimens.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Was ASTM D618 moisture conditioning applied? What was the conditioning protocol?"
- "Is crystallinity measured by DSC or XRD, not just inferred from processing?"
- "For recyclability claims, is MFI measured after each reprocessing cycle?"
- "Are specimens taken from consistent locations and orientations relative to flow direction?"
