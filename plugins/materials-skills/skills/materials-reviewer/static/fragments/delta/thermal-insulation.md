# Thermal Insulation — Reviewer Delta
# Adds to the civil-template. Load civil.md first, then this file.

## Domain-specific review axes

- **Measurement method consistency**: Thermal conductivity values from different methods (hot-plate, hot-wire,
  laser flash, heat-flow meter) CANNOT be directly compared. Method and mean temperature must both be stated.
- **Density dependence**: Compressive strength and conductivity are both density-dependent.
  All performance comparisons require density normalization or matching density groups.
- **Fire performance**: Fire reaction data (ASTM E84 / EN 13501-1) requires specimen geometry
  and test standard. LOI alone is insufficient for fire-safety claims.
- **Hygrothermal aging**: Hydrophobicity claims require water contact angle AND moisture uptake data,
  not surface morphology alone.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- Conductivity values from different methods compared without noting the method difference.
- Compressive strength without specimen density — cannot compare lightweight vs dense grades.
- Fire-resistance claim from LOI only — LOI does not characterize flame spread or smoke.
- Conductivity at room temperature only for a material intended for elevated-temperature service.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "What measurement method and mean temperature were used for thermal conductivity?"
- "Is density reported for every specimen? Are comparisons made at matched densities?"
- "Is fire performance measured by standardized fire test (ASTM E84 or EN 13501-1)?"
- "Is conductivity measured across the operating temperature range, not just room temperature?"
