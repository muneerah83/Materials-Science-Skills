## Domain-specific review axes

- **J-V scan direction**: Both forward AND reverse scan required to assess hysteresis.
  Perovskite and organic solar cells show strong hysteresis; forward-only scan overestimates PCE.
- **EQE spectral measurement**: Full spectral EQE curve required alongside PCE.
  A single PCE value without EQE cannot assess wavelength-selective absorption or loss mechanisms.
- **Standard test conditions**: AM1.5G spectrum and 100 mW/cm² illumination must be confirmed.
  Non-standard measurement conditions (different intensity, spectrum) cannot be compared to literature.
- **Integrating sphere calibration**: Absolute PLQY measurement requires integrating sphere calibration.
  Relative PL intensity cannot substitute for absolute quantum yield.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- PCE from forward scan only — hysteresis not characterized.
- Efficiency without spectral EQE — loss mechanisms unknown.
- Solar simulator conditions not confirmed (AM1.5G spectrum, light intensity).
- Absolute PLQY claimed without integrating sphere.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Are both forward and reverse J-V scans provided? What is the hysteresis index?"
- "Is the full spectral EQE curve provided across the absorption range?"
- "Is the solar simulator spectrum and intensity certified (AM1.5G, 100 mW/cm²)?"
- "Is absolute PLQY from integrating sphere, or relative PL intensity only?"
