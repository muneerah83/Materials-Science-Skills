# Timber, Masonry, and Bamboo — Reviewer Delta
# Adds to the civil-template. Load civil.md first, then this file.

## Domain-specific review axes

- **Grain direction**: Properties must be reported both parallel AND perpendicular to grain.
  Single-direction data misrepresents the anisotropy of wood-based materials.
- **Moisture content**: Moisture content at time of testing must be stated.
  Mechanical properties of timber vary 30–50% between air-dry and saturated conditions.
- **Connection failure mode**: Joint/connection tests must identify failure mode
  (member failure vs. joint/fastener failure) — strength without failure mode is uninterpretable.
- **Species and grade**: Wood species and structural grade must be stated for mechanical data.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- Strength reported for one grain direction only — anisotropy not characterized.
- Moisture content at testing not stated.
- Connection strength reported without failure mode identification.
- Durability claim from short-term wetting only — no dimensional stability curve.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Are properties reported both parallel and perpendicular to grain?"
- "What was the moisture content at time of testing?"
- "What was the failure mode in the connection test?"
- "What species/grade or bamboo culm position is tested?"
