# High-Temperature Alloys and Superalloys — Reviewer Delta
# Adds to the metals-template. Load metals.md first, then this file.

## Domain-specific review axes

- **Creep regime completeness**: Creep data must show primary, secondary (steady-state), and tertiary regimes.
  Rupture life alone without minimum creep rate cannot support creep-resistance claims.
- **Larson-Miller extrapolation**: Time-temperature extrapolation to service life requires Larson-Miller
  validation. Short-duration tests extrapolated across orders of magnitude in time are not accepted.
- **γ/γ′ strengthening evidence**: TEM evidence of precipitate size, volume fraction, and coherency required.
  Microstructural stability at service temperature must be demonstrated, not just as-processed state.
- **Oxidation cycling**: Cyclic oxidation data (not just isothermal TGA) required for oxidation-resistance claims.
  Spallation behavior during thermal cycling is the critical failure mode.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- Rupture life without minimum creep rate or steady-state regime identification.
- γ′ strengthening claimed without TEM precipitate size/fraction evidence.
- Isothermal oxidation only — cyclic spallation not assessed.
- Larson-Miller extrapolation applied beyond the validated temperature-time range.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Are all three creep regimes (primary, secondary, tertiary) identified?"
- "What is the minimum creep rate at the reported stress and temperature?"
- "Is γ′ precipitate size, volume fraction, and coherency measured by TEM?"
- "Is cyclic oxidation data provided alongside isothermal TGA?"
