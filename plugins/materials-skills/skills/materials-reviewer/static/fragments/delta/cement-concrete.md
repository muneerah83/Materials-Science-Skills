# Adds to the civil-template. Load civil.md first, then this file.

## Domain-specific review axes

- **Hydration product identification**: "Denser C-S-H" claims require MIP or NMR evidence, not SEM alone.
  TGA quantification of portlandite (450°C peak) and carbonates (600–800°C) is standard.
- **Curing conditions**: Temperature, RH, and age at testing must all be stated.
  Comparing 28-day strengths from different curing conditions is invalid.
- **Sustainability**: "Green" or "low-carbon" claims require LCA with a defined functional unit and system boundary.
  CO₂ reduction percentages without a reference system are unverifiable.
- **Pore structure**: Chloride penetration and freeze-thaw durability claims require MIP or RCPT (ASTM C1202) data,
  not just strength data as a proxy.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- Strength comparison across different w/c or curing ages without normalisation.
- Attributing strength gain to "C-S-H densification" without XRD/TGA/MIP evidence.
- LCA claim without defined functional unit, system boundary, and CO₂ source.
- No pore structure data for durability claims.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Are curing temperature, RH, and age all specified for every test series?"
- "What XRD, TGA, or NMR evidence supports the hydration product attribution?"
- "Is the LCA functional unit defined and the system boundary stated (cradle-to-gate)?"
- "Is chloride permeability quantified by RCPT or diffusion coefficient?"
