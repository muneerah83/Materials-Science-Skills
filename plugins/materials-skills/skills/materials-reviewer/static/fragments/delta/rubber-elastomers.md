# Rubbers and Elastomers — Reviewer Delta

## Domain-specific review axes

- **Elastic recovery and compression set**: For sealing, vibration isolation, or gasket claims,
  compression set (ASTM D395) and elastic recovery data are required alongside tensile properties.
  Tensile strength alone does not characterize seal performance.
- **Ozone resistance**: Requires dedicated ozone aging test (ASTM D1149 / ISO 1431-1).
  Heat aging (ASTM D573) alone cannot substitute for ozone resistance assessment.
- **Crosslink density from DMA or swelling**: DMA rubbery plateau modulus or equilibrium swelling
  measurement is required. Shore hardness alone does not quantify crosslink density.
- **Filler dispersion**: SEM/TEM evidence of filler dispersion is required before attributing
  property gains to nanoscale reinforcement. Low-magnification SEM is insufficient.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- Sealing performance claimed from tensile strength only — compression set not measured.
- Ozone resistance asserted from heat aging data alone.
- Crosslink density inferred from hardness only — DMA or swelling not performed.
- Filler reinforcement attributed without dispersion evidence.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Is compression set (ASTM D395) measured for sealing or isolation applications?"
- "Is ozone aging (ASTM D1149) performed separately from heat aging?"
- "Is crosslink density from DMA rubbery plateau or equilibrium swelling?"
- "Is filler dispersion characterized by TEM/SEM with adequate magnification?"
