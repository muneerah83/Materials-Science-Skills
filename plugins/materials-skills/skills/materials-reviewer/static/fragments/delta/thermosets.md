# Thermosetting Polymers — Reviewer Delta
# Adds to the polymers-template. Load polymers.md first, then this file.

## Domain-specific review axes

- **Degree of cure**: DSC residual heat AND FTIR functional group conversion both required.
  Claiming "fully cured" from Tg alone without residual exotherm measurement is insufficient.
- **Crosslink density**: DMA rubbery plateau modulus (E' in the plateau region) is the correct metric.
  Inferring crosslink density from Tg alone is not accepted — Tg depends on both mobility and crosslinking.
- **Curing schedule completeness**: Temperature, time, and stoichiometric ratio (resin:hardener)
  must all be stated. Missing stoichiometry makes the system irreproducible.
- **Adhesion and interfacial bond**: For structural adhesive claims, failure mode (cohesive / adhesive /
  substrate) from lap-shear or butt-joint test is required.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- "Fully cured" claimed from Tg alone without DSC residual heat or FTIR conversion.
- Crosslink density from Tg only — DMA rubbery plateau not measured.
- Stoichiometric ratio of resin:hardener not reported.
- Adhesive performance without failure mode identification.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Is degree of cure quantified by DSC residual heat AND FTIR epoxy conversion?"
- "Is crosslink density from DMA rubbery plateau modulus (not Tg alone)?"
- "Is the stoichiometric ratio (resin:hardener ratio) stated for all formulations?"
- "What is the failure mode in the adhesion test: cohesive, adhesive, or substrate?"
