## Domain-specific review axes

- **Fiber direction completeness**: 0° tensile data alone is insufficient.
  90° transverse and ±45° shear data required to characterize laminate anisotropy.
- **Fiber volume fraction measurement**: Nominal fiber content cannot substitute for measured
  fiber volume fraction (ASTM D3171 acid digestion or burn-off). Properties must be normalised.
- **Interface characterization**: ILSS (ASTM D2344) and SEM fracture surface (fiber pull-out vs.
  debonding failure mode) are required for any fiber-matrix interface optimization claim.
- **Fatigue S-N curve**: At least 3–4 stress levels with multiple specimens per level.
  Single-stress-level fatigue data cannot establish an S-N relationship.

## Domain-specific fatal flaws

- Single fiber orientation data — 90° and shear not characterized.
- Fiber volume fraction nominal only — not measured and verified.
- Interface improvement claimed without ILSS data or fracture surface SEM.
- Fatigue life at one stress level presented as an S-N relationship.

## Domain-specific reviewer questions

- "Are 0°, 90°, and shear (±45°) properties all measured?"
- "Is fiber volume fraction measured (ASTM D3171), not just nominal?"
- "What is the failure mode in the ILSS test: interlaminar or flexural?"
- "How many stress levels and how many specimens per level for the S-N curve?"
